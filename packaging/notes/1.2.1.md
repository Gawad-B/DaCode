DaCode 1.2.1: the terminal on the Mac, page keys, smoother scrolling, and an updater that shows its progress.

If you have 1.1.0 or later, DaCode offers this release itself — and from now on it shows how much has
downloaded, then asks to restart in a popup.

## What is new

- **The integrated terminal, and ▶ Run, are in the Mac build.** 1.2.0 left them out because a
  shell could outlive its tab there; that is fixed, and nine full test runs on macOS were clean.
- **PageUp and PageDown** move a screen at a time, the view with the caret. They did nothing before.
- **Home finds the code** without Vim's keys: the first character of the line's code, then its start.
- **The update shows its progress** as a bar and "12.3 of 25.3 MB", and a finished download asks to
  restart in a popup instead of waiting in the bell's panel.

## Fixed

- Holding an arrow at the edge of the view flickered, as if the page were reloading.
- Characters ran into each other — `=` drawn over the end of the name before it.
- Opening the bell's panel stuttered.

The full list is in `CHANGELOG.md`.
